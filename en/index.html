FAQ

The questions we get asked.

What is Vector?

Vector is an AI-powered planning app built for solopreneurs. Arthur, your Chief Planning Officer, turns your scattered tasks into a clear day plan — what to do, in what order, how long — slotted into your calendar. The AI plans, you accomplish.

Who is Vector for?

For overwhelmed solopreneurs, consultants, creators, coaches and freelancers who want to start each morning with a clear plan — without having to decide everything.

What is Arthur?

Arthur is Vector's AI agent — your Chief Planning Officer (CPO). He estimates each task's real duration, prioritizes by your deadlines, slots your week into your calendar, and re-plans on his own when something unexpected comes up.

How is Vector different from Notion, Todoist or Motion?

Those tools manage lists or calendars; Vector turns your list into a concrete plan and learns your real durations over time. No heavy maintenance, no corporate interface — a calm copilot built for solos.

How much does it cost?

The Foundation plan is free. The Acceleration plan, with the full Arthur, starts at US$35/month and includes a free 14-day trial, no credit card required.

Is my data protected?

Yes. Your data belongs to you, can be exported anytime, and is never sold.
