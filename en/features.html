FAQ

Frequently asked questions about features

Which Vector features are free?

The Foundation plan is free, forever. It includes Vector's automatic planning engine: calendar planning, forecasting and learning of real task durations, availability management, unlimited categories, tasks and subtasks, recurring tasks, plus the List, Gantt and Calendar views.

What consumes AI credits in Vector?

Arthur's intelligence features consume credits: building a complete project, planning a segment, breaking down a task, extracting tasks, feasibility checks, integrating a constraint and workload rebalancing. The Acceleration plan includes 100 credits per month; on any plan, you can add credit packs. The morning and weekly briefings are included without consuming credits.

Does Vector have a Gantt view?

Yes. Vector offers a Gantt view that shows the cascading dependencies and critical path of your project. When you move a task or change its duration, all its dependencies follow automatically. The Gantt view is available from the free Foundation plan.

What's the difference between Foundation and Acceleration?

Foundation (free) gives you the automatic planning engine and pay-as-you-go access to the essential AI features, paid with credits. Acceleration (US$35/month) adds Arthur's full strategic planning, unlimited projects, 100 AI credits included every month, the daily morning briefing, and the proactive watch that sets your projects straight before they derail.

Does Vector learn how long my tasks take?

Yes. As you complete your tasks, Vector learns your real pace. Its duration estimates stop being generic and become yours, sharpening over time.

Can Vector pull tasks from my emails or notes?

Yes. Paste an email, a meeting recap or some notes, and Arthur extracts the tasks, deadlines and the right project. Direct Outlook and Gmail calendar integration, along with scanning your conversations with an external AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini), are coming soon.

Does Vector have a mobile app?

A mobile app is in development and coming soon. It will let you capture a task wherever it hits you: in bed at 11 p.m., walking out of a client meeting, or at the grocery store.
