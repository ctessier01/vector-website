Between:

1. Definitions

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any non-public information relating to Vector, disclosed in any form — written, oral, visual, electronic or otherwise — including in particular:

  • features of the application not yet publicly announced;
  • the user interface (layouts, screen flows, screenshots);
  • the architecture of the AI agents, their prompts and their operating logic;
  • the duration-estimation and planning algorithms;
  • the database structure and schemas;
  • observed bugs, limitations and performance;
  • internal benchmarks, performance tests and technical analyses;
  • the roadmap, pricing plans in development, and commercial and marketing strategies;
  • any other information identified as confidential or reasonably understood to be so at the time of its disclosure.

1.2 "Beta Program" means the testing phase preceding Vector's public launch, during which you receive free or preferential-rate access in exchange for your feedback.

1.3 "Feedback" means any comment, suggestion, idea, bug report or other feedback you provide to us about Vector.

1.4 "Trade Secrets" means the subset of Confidential Information consisting in particular of source code, proprietary prompts, AI-agent architecture, estimation and planning algorithms, prioritization logic, database schemas, and any other non-public technical element whose disclosure could cause lasting harm to Vector.

3.1 Confidential Information. You acknowledge that, in the context of the Beta Program, you will have access to Confidential Information whose disclosure could harm Vector.

4. Tester obligations

4.1 Non-disclosure. You undertake not to disclose the Confidential Information to any third party whatsoever. Your participation in the Beta Program is strictly personal: the Confidential Information must not be shared, shown or made accessible to anyone other than you.

4.2 Limited use. You will use the Confidential Information solely to participate in the Beta Program and provide your Feedback.

4.3 No public screenshots or demonstrations. You will not publish screenshots, videos, demonstrations or detailed descriptions of the interface or features on channels accessible to the public or to an indeterminate number of people — including social media, blogs, public forums, mailing lists, video platforms, presentations to an audience, conferences or open online groups — without our prior written consent. 4.4 No use of the Confidential Information to develop a competing product. You will not attempt to copy, duplicate or recreate Vector or its components using the Confidential Information, nor reverse-engineer it, nor use that information as a basis to develop a competing product. This clause does not prevent you from independently developing a product in the same field, provided you do not rely on the Confidential Information to do so.

4.5 No public benchmarks. You will not publish performance tests, benchmarks, comparative analyses, technical audits or detailed evaluations of Vector without our prior written consent. This restriction does not prevent you from sharing your general opinion under the conditions set out in section 5.

4.6 No access sharing. Your account is strictly personal. You will not share your credentials or your access with a third party, and you will not give anyone else the ability to view or use the Beta Program.

4.7 Reasonable security. You will take reasonable measures to protect the Confidential Information (strong password, secure device, logging out when sharing a device).

4.8 Breach notification. You undertake to notify Vector immediately, as soon as you become aware of any actual or reasonably suspected breach of this agreement, without waiting, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours at the latest, including any compromise of your account, accidental disclosure of Confidential Information, loss or theft of a device, or disclosure request received from a third party.

4.9 Disclosure required by law. If you are legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information (court order, subpoena, regulatory requirement or other legal obligation), you must, unless legally prohibited: (a) notify Vector without delay and before the disclosure; (b) cooperate reasonably to allow Vector to seek a protective order or other appropriate relief; and (c) disclose only the portion strictly required by law. A disclosure made in accordance with this section does not constitute a breach of this agreement.
